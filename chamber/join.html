Non-Profit (NP) Membership Benefits

Cost: No Fee (Verified 501(c)(3) or registered NGOs only)

  • Standard listing in the online Chamber Business Directory
  • Access to monthly community networking mixers
  • Subscription to the Chamber newsletter & policy updates
  • Eligibility to post community volunteer opportunities

Bronze Membership Benefits

Cost: $250 / year

  • All Non-Profit Membership benefits
  • Enhanced directory listing with company logo and website link
  • 10% discount on Chamber workshops and business training
  • Ribbon-cutting ceremony announcement on Chamber social media

Silver Membership Benefits

Cost: $500 / year

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • 25% discount on all Chamber events, expos, and training seminars
  • Quarterly rotating spotlight feature on the Chamber Home Page
  • Access to exclusive Business-to-Business (B2B) lead generation groups
  • 2 complimentary tickets to the Annual Chamber Gala

Gold Membership Benefits

Cost: $1,000 / year

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Permanent premium spotlight banner placement on the Home Page
  • 50% discount on booth space at the Annual Regional Trade Show
  • VIP seating and speaking opportunities at Chamber luncheons
  • Dedicated business consultation and advocacy representation
