W05 Project

Wisdom Educational Academy

Website Site Plan

Adama City, Ethiopia

1. Site Name

Wisdom Educational Academy

I selected this name because it represents the school's identity and its commitment to helping students gain knowledge, develop good character, and prepare for a successful future. The name is clear and familiar to parents, students, teachers, and the local community.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Wisdom Educational Academy website is to provide a reliable online place where parents, students, teachers, and visitors can learn about the school and access important school information.

  • Introduce the school, its vision, mission, and educational services on the home page.
  • Provide details about grade levels and educational programs on a programs page.
  • Display school announcements and activities on a news page, with information loaded dynamically from a local JSON data file.
  • Provide an admission or contact form so parents and visitors can send an inquiry.

3. Scenarios

These questions represent information that the website's target audience may look for.

Scenario 1: A parent looking for a school

What grade levels does Wisdom Educational Academy offer, and how can I learn about the admission process?

Scenario 2: A parent of a current student

Where can I find the latest school announcements, academic calendar, and information about student activities?

Scenario 3: A visitor or community member

Where is the school located, and how can I contact the school office?

4. Color Schema

The website will use a simple, professional color scheme that supports readability and reflects trust and achievement.

Dark blue — #1B3A57

Used for the header, main headings, navigation, and footer. It communicates trust and professionalism.

Gold — #F4B400

Used for accent lines, buttons, highlights, and important links. It adds energy and draws attention.

White — #FFFFFF

Used for the main content background and text placed on dark blue areas to keep the design clean and readable.

Light gray — #F3F5F7

Used for section backgrounds and information cards to separate content without making the page feel crowded.

5. Typography

Poppins — headings

Poppins will be used for the site title, page headings, and section headings. Its clear, modern letterforms make headings easy to recognize.

Roboto — body text

Roboto will be used for paragraphs, lists, navigation labels, and other general content because it is readable on both mobile phones and larger screens.
